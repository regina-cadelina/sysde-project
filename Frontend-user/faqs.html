Frequently Asked Questions

How can I place an order?

You can browse our products on the website and submit your order through our order form. We'll confirm details with you before processing.

Do you accept rush orders?

Yes, we accept rush orders depending on our current workload. Please include your required date during checkout or contact us directly.

How do I send my design?

You can upload your file when placing an order or email it to us after submitting your order form.

Can I pay online?

Payments are handled offline via GCash, bank transfer, or cash upon pickup. Details will be provided after we confirm your order.

Where are you located?

We are located in Batangas City, Philippines. Pick-up instructions will be provided after your order is confirmed.
